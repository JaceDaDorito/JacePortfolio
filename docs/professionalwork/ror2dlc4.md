# Risk of Rain 2: Hallowed Concepts

|Studio|Role|Engine|Dates|
|----|----|----|----|
| Dream Toaster LLC |Level Designer & Technical Artist| Unity 3D | 2026 |

## Project Summary
Hallowed Concepts is Risk of Rain 2's fourth expansion exploring N'kuhana's influence on the planet. The expansion focuses on introducing new risk-reward systems such as the Hexes, Trials, and N'tormat.

## Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and balance a majority of the new Trials.
- Create new systems to pair with the new Trials (Pickup Cleaner Wave, Player Teleport Overrides, Monster Pool Overrides, Player Level Syncing, etc.)
- Create new Levels for the Trials (Trial of the Conduit & the Trial Completion Tower).
- Create a large portion of visual effects for each of the Trials.
- Create UX elements for the Trials' objectives & "Reckoning" timer.
- Create the main visual setpieces & skybox for the Echo of Providence fight.
- Create a quick prototype level for the Echo of Providence fight.  
- Storyboard an initial draft of the Echo of Providence cutscene.
- Implement and balance new DLC4 monsters into existing stages.
- Make monster pools for the new DLC4 maps.
- Bug fixes for new levels and Trials.
