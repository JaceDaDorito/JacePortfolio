## My Role As A Developer
I was brought onto the team primarily as a level designer decently far into the expansion's development. A lot of my efforts have been put into learning the current state of the expansion and documenting them. The main objective given to me was to catch up with and expand the pending DLC3 levels in a lightweight manner. I was largely responsible for concepting or creating new level mechanics to make the DLC3 progression path feel special. Eventually, I branched out into creating a large chunk of the visual sequences present in the expansion since that was something I was familiar with in my personal work.

## Responsibilities
- Provide complete feedback design documents and graphics on each new map.
- Integrate and balance new content pieces (enemies, interactables, and drones) on existing and new maps.
- Design and implement various gameplay progression elements into new maps (Conduit Canyon and Computational Exchange).
- Implement elements of DLC3 progression.
- Direct and implement various visual sequences.
- Various miscellaneous visual effects.
- Assistance in bug fixing levels and visual sequences.
