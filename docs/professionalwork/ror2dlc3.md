## About Project
Alloyed Collective is Risk of Rain 2's third expansion coming off the heals of Seekers of the Storm.

## My Role As A Developer
 Dream Toaster reached out to me in the winter of 2024 seeking my help with the upcoming Risk of Rain 2 DLC: Alloyed Collective. They had discovered me through my reputation in the modding community, where I created a popular custom level that not only fit the art style and gameplay of RoR2, but also introduced underwater level mechanics that dynamically shifted the gravity of every character on the map.
 
After onboarding, I began by conducting an in-depth analysis of the upcoming DLC levels. I wrote documentation of the individual landmarks, secrets, and enemy compositions of each of the levels.  Afterwards, I gave my opinion of each level and what I would do with each. Eventually, I started designing and implementing a lot of my pitches; most notably, I designed the progression for Conduit Canyon. I was also largely responsible for implementing the individual progression beats for the expansion's special level path.

Though I was onboarded for level design, I also branched out into creating a lot of the expansion's visual sequences, some of which were the progression visuals for Conduit Canyon and sequences relating to the true final boss.

## Responsibilities
- Provide complete feedback design documents and graphics on each new map.
- Integrate and balance new content pieces (enemies, interactables, and drones) on existing and new maps.
- Design and implement various gameplay elements into new maps (Conduit Canyon and Computational Exchange).
- Implement elements of DLC3 progression.
- Direct and implement various visual sequences (expansion endings, true final boss introduction sequences, etc.)
- Various miscellaneous visual effects.
- Assistance in bug fixing levels and visual sequences.
