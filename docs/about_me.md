# About Me

Hello and thank you for visiting my website!

I'm Jason Torres, but a lot of people call me Jace. I am primarily a level designer and gameplay programmer, but I also love to draw!

I've had an infatuation with video games for as long as I remember, a lot of my childhood consists of playing Wii games like Super Mario Galaxy and early steam games like Terraria. My love for level design was born from playing custom levels for some Super Mario game that ran on Adobe Flash, I do not recall which game it was specifically. Unfortunately because of that love, I subjected my mom to try to figure out how to mod my Wii console to no avail (sorry mom).

When entering high school, I started participating in developing various Terraria mods. I did not know how to program then, but I did start learning how to draw and do pixel art. I met a lot of very artistic people during that time, a lot of whom I still frequently talk to today! During this time, I have grown an appreciation for the arts and a fondness towards environmental design.

Near the tail end of high school and during college, I learned how to program in C# by modding various Unity games. The main game I modded was Risk of Rain 2. It started off as creating simple sound replacements, but I eventually wanted to learn how to create custom levels for the game. Modded levels were always possible to make, but the tooling was underdeveloped and the there was no documentation. A lot of my early modded levels consisted of [tests](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7nrP0p8m8ao) and [level prototypes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIbm7Bc0fi4).

<img src="/img/ror2_custom_level_test.jpg" alt="Selfie at gearbox" width="700"/>

 Come fall of 2022, there was an announcement in the Risk of Rain 2 modding Discord server to host a [Halloween Modjam](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/?includedCategories=327). I decided that I wanted to submit a custom level for the jam; that level would come to be <a href="./personalwork/fblmod/">Fogbound Lagoon</a>. Said mod paved the way to the creation of modded level creation documentation and got me noticed by Dream Toaster to work on Risk of Rain 2 directly. I am now a seasoned developer with a shipped title under my belt!
